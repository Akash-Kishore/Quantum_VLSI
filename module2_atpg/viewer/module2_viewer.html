Fault model: The AB-term Toffoli gate is stuck-at-0 — its contribution never reaches the Cout XOR accumulator. The faulty circuit computes Cout = BC ⊕ AC instead of the correct AB ⊕ BC ⊕ AC.

Detection: The fault is detected whenever the true AB product is 1, i.e. whenever A=1 AND B=1, regardless of Cin. This gives exactly 2 fault-detecting inputs out of 8.

Note: Sum is not computed in this circuit — the fault does not affect Sum, so including it would add ancillas for zero benefit. This was a deliberate design decision, not an omission.
